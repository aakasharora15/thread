one line, no gaps

Draw a single line that fills every square and touches the numbers in order. Two hundred boards, three lanes. Every board has exactly one answer.

Medium

CandyLevels 1 to 10
How it works
  1. Start on 1 and finish on the highest number.
  2. Move between squares that share an edge. No diagonals.
  3. Fill every open square exactly once. No gaps, no crossings.
  4. Reach the numbers in order.

Thick dark bars are walls, and the line cannot cross them. Drag back along the line to rub it out.

On Medium and Hard you are free to go the wrong way, so you can wall yourself in or take a number out of turn. If the line runs out of room, the board is lost and you start it again. Retries are free and unlimited. Easy keeps a safety net: it refuses any move that would strand a square or skip a number.

Three dots per level: one for solving it, one for solving it without rubbing anything out, one for doing that inside the target time. Target times are provisional and not yet set from playtest data.
