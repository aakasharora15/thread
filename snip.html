Snip & StitchPhysics

Cut the strings in the right order and let the spool swing, fall and thread the needle's eye.

How it works
  1. Drag across a string to cut it.
  2. Gravity does the rest: the spool swings from whatever still holds it.
  3. Land the spool in the gold ring to finish the level.

Red bars are blades. Touch one and the spool is lost. Let it fall off the screen and the level restarts. Retries are free and unlimited.
