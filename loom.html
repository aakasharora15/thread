Loom LogicGeometry

Wind the thread from peg to peg until the shape you have woven matches the blueprint underneath.

How it works
  1. The dashed line is the blueprint. Your thread starts on the first peg.
  2. Tap a peg to run the thread to it.
  3. Tap the peg you are currently on to unwind the last strand.

Finish on the last peg of the blueprint with every strand in place. Weaving it in reverse counts too. Undo as often as you like, or start the level over from the button in the corner.
